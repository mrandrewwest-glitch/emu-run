Emu Run — Privacy

Emu Run is a running game: you're an emu legging it across the outback with a dingo on your tail. Here's exactly what it does and doesn't do with anything about the person playing it.

No accounts, no ads, no purchases

There's nothing to sign up for, nothing to buy, and no advertising of any kind in the game.

No data leaves the device

The app makes no network calls while you play. Your high scores, the name you type in for the high score board, and whether sound is on are saved only on your own device and are never sent anywhere. There's no analytics and no tracking.

No microphone, camera or location

Emu Run doesn't use the microphone, camera, contacts, photos or your location.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it saved.
